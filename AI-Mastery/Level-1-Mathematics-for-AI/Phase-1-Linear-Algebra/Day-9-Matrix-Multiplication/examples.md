# Examples — Matrix Multiplication

## Example 1 — Very Easy
2x2 Square Multiplication: $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 8 \\ 7 & 7 \end{bmatrix}$.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Rectangular Multiplication: $(2 \times 3) \cdot (3 \times 2) = (2 \times 2)$ shape output.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Matrix-Vector Multiplication: Matrix $\mathbf{A}_{2 \times 2}$ times Column Vector $\mathbf{x}_{2 \times 1}$ yields Column Vector $\mathbf{y}_{2 \times 1}$.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Non-Commutativity Proof: $\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \mathbf{B} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \implies \mathbf{A}\mathbf{B} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \mathbf{B}\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Neural Network Forward Batch: Batch size 4, 3 inputs, 2 outputs. $\mathbf{X}_{4 \times 3} \mathbf{W}_{3 \times 2} + \mathbf{b}_{1 \times 2} = \mathbf{Y}_{4 \times 2}$.
