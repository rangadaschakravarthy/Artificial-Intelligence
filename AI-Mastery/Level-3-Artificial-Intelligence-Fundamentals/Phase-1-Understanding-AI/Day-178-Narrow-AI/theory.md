# Day 178 Theory: Narrow Artificial Intelligence

### 1. What Is It?
Narrow AI (also known as Weak AI or Artificial Narrow Intelligence - ANI) refers to AI systems designed and trained to perform a specific, single task or narrow range of tasks with high efficiency or superhuman accuracy.

### 2. Key Characteristics
- **Domain Bound**: Operates strictly within predefined feature parameters and task rules.
- **Zero Transfer**: Intelligence cannot automatically generalize or transfer to a different domain without complete retraining or re-engineering.
- **Current Reality**: 100% of deployed commercial AI systems today (from spam filters to AlphaFold) are Narrow AI systems.

### 3. Real-World Examples
1. **AlphaGo**: Defeated world champion Go players, but cannot play Tic-Tac-Toe or drive a car.
2. **Medical Diagnostics**: Detects lung tumors from CT scans with 98% accuracy, but cannot parse a clinical PDF note.

### 4. Summary
Narrow AI delivers high domain-specific capability but lacks general task transferability.
