# Day 178 — Narrow AI

## Learning Objectives
- Define Narrow AI (Weak AI) and analyze why all current commercial AI systems belong to this category.
- Examine real-world Narrow AI implementations (Spam filtering, Speech recognition, Medical image analysis).
- Understand the domain-boundary brittle limitations of Narrow AI.

## Prerequisites
- Day 174: What is Artificial Intelligence

## Topics Covered
- Definition of Narrow AI (Artificial Narrow Intelligence - ANI)
- Domain Specificity: High performance within a single narrow goal, zero transfer capability outside domain
- Examples: AlphaGo, Siri/Alexa, Cancer detection vision, Fraud detection systems
- Brittleness: Why a superhuman Chess bot cannot play Checkers without total reprogramming
- Economic impact and dominance of ANI in current industry

## Difficulty
Beginner / Intermediate
