# Day 201 Theory: Second AI Winter (1987–1993)

### 1. What Is It?
The Second AI Winter was a commercial and academic crash from 1987 to 1993, driven by the collapse of the specialized LISP machine industry and the maintenance failure of hand-coded expert systems.

### 2. Core Catalyst Factors
1. **Hardware Obsolescence**: Specialized LISP machines ($100,000+ per unit) were rendered obsolete by cheaper, faster general-purpose x86 IBM PCs and Sun Microsystems Unix workstations.
2. **Brittleness & Maintenance Cost**: Updating 10,000+ hand-written rules in enterprise expert systems caused unexpected rule conflicts, making maintenance prohibitively expensive.

### 3. Summary
The Second AI Winter was caused by commodity x86 hardware outperforming expensive Lisp machines, and the maintenance collapse of hand-coded rule bases.
