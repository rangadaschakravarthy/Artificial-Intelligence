# Day 193 — AI Before Modern AI & Early Ideas

## Learning Objectives
- Trace historical philosophical and mathematical roots of artificial intelligence before modern digital computers.
- Examine mechanical calculators, formal logic (Aristotle, Leibniz, Boole), and automata.
- Understand the transition from mechanical calculation to formal symbol manipulation.

## Prerequisites
- Days 173–192 (Phase 1 Complete)

## Topics Covered
- Ancient Automata and Myths (Talos, Mechanical Ducks)
- Leibniz's *Alphabet of Human Thought* & *Characteristica Universalis*
- George Boole and Boolean Algebra (Formalizing logic as 0 and 1)
- Charles Babbage and Ada Lovelace (Analytical Engine & first algorithms)
- The shift from numerical calculation to symbolic operational processing

## Difficulty
Beginner / Intermediate
