# Examples — Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

## Example 1 — Very Easy
Diagonal Matrix Eigenvalues: 

$$\mathbf{D} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 0 & -7 \end{bmatrix} \implies \lambda_1 = 3, \mathbf{v}_1=[1,0]^T; \lambda_2 = -7, \mathbf{v}_2=[0,1]^T$$

.

## Example 2 — Beginner
2x2 Eigenvalues: 

$$\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \implies \lambda_1 = 5, \mathbf{v}_1=[1,1]^T; \lambda_2 = 2, \mathbf{v}_2=[1,-2]^T$$

.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Trace and Det Check: $\text{Tr}(\mathbf{A}) = 4+3 = 7 = 5+2$. $\det(\mathbf{A}) = 12-2 = 10 = 5 \times 2$. Matches!

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
PageRank Eigenvector: Dominant eigenvector $(\lambda=1)$ of web link transition probability matrix.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
PCA Variance: Largest eigenvalue $\lambda_1$ equals maximum variance captured along principal component 1.
