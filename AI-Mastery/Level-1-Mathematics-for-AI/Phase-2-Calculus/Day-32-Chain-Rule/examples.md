# Examples — Chain Rule

## Example 1 — Very Easy
Single-Variable Chain Rule: $y = (2x + 5)^3 \implies y' = 3(2x+5)^2 \cdot 2 = 6(2x+5)^2$.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Exponential Chain Rule: $y = e^{x^2} \implies y' = e^{x^2} \cdot (2x) = 2x e^{x^2}$.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Logarithmic Chain Rule: $y = \ln(x^2 + 1) \implies y' = \frac{1}{x^2+1} \cdot 2x = \frac{2x}{x^2+1}$.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Neural Layer Gradient: $\frac{d}{dw}[\text{ReLU}(w x + b)] = \text{ReLU}'(w x + b) \cdot x$.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Multi-Path Computational Graph: Summing chain derivatives over multiple intermediate paths.
