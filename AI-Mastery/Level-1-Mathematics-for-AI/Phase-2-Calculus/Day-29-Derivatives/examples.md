# Examples — Derivatives

## Example 1 — Very Easy
Power Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}[x^4] = 4x^3$. At $x=2$, $4(8) = 32$.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Exponential Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}[e^x] = e^x$. At $x=0$, $e^0 = 1$.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Logarithm Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}[\ln(x)] = \frac{1}{x}$. At $x=5$, $1/5 = 0.2$.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Trigonometric Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}[\sin(x)] = \cos(x)$. At $x=0$, $\cos(0) = 1$.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Central Difference: Numerically calculating derivative in Python using $h=10^{-5}$.
