# Day 196 — Dartmouth Conference

## Learning Objectives
- Explore the 1956 Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence.
- Understand how John McCarthy coined the term "Artificial Intelligence".
- Analyze the founding proposal conjecture and key founding figures.

## Prerequisites
- Day 193: AI Before Modern AI & Early Ideas

## Topics Covered
- Dartmouth Conference (Summer 1956, Dartmouth College)
- Key Organizers: John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester, Claude Shannon
- Coining the term "Artificial Intelligence"
- The Core Dartmouth Proposal Conjecture:
  > "Every aspect of learning or any other feature of intelligence can in principle be so precisely described that a machine can be made to simulate it."
- The birth of AI as an official academic research field

## Difficulty
Beginner / Intermediate
