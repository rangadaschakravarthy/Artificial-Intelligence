# Day 204 Theory: Deep Learning Revolution

### 1. What Is It?
The Deep Learning Revolution refers to the dramatic breakthrough starting in 2012 where deep artificial neural networks, powered by GPUs and massive datasets, outperformed traditional feature-engineered computer vision and speech systems.

### 2. The ImageNet 2012 Milestone
In the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC 2012), **AlexNet** achieved a top-5 error rate of 15.3%, beating the runner-up classical feature-engineered system (26.2%) by more than 10 percentage points—demonstrating the power of deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).

### 3. Summary
The 2012 ImageNet AlexNet breakthrough proved that deep neural networks trained on GPUs automatically learn features superior to human-engineered features.
