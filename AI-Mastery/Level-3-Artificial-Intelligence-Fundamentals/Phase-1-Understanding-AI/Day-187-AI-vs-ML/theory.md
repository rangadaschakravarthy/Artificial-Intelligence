# Day 187 Theory: Artificial Intelligence vs Machine Learning

### 1. What Is It?
Artificial Intelligence is the broad umbrella discipline dedicated to building intelligent systems. Machine Learning is a specific paradigm *within* AI where systems learn decision functions automatically from data experience.

### 2. Venn Diagram Relationship

$$
\text{Artificial Intelligence (AI)}
$$

$$
\text{├── Non-ML AI: Symbolic Logic, Graph Search (A*), Expert Systems, Rule Engines}
$$

$$
\text{└── Machine Learning (ML): Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning}
$$

### 3. Summary
All Machine Learning is AI, but not all AI is Machine Learning. Search algorithms (A*) and Expert Systems are AI without ML.
