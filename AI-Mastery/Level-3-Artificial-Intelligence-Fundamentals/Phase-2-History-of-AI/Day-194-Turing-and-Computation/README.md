# Day 194 — Turing and Computation

## Learning Objectives
- Understand Alan Turing's fundamental contributions to computer science and artificial intelligence.
- Master the theoretical model of the Universal Turing Machine.
- Connect computability theory to mechanical intelligence.

## Prerequisites
- Day 193: AI Before Modern AI & Early Ideas

## Topics Covered
- Alan Turing's 1936 paper: *On Computable Numbers*
- Universal Turing Machine (Tape, Read/Write Head, State Transition Table)
- The Church-Turing Thesis (Any algorithmically computable function can be executed by a Turing Machine)
- The Halting Problem & Undecidability
- Turing's 1950 paper: *Computing Machinery and Intelligence*
