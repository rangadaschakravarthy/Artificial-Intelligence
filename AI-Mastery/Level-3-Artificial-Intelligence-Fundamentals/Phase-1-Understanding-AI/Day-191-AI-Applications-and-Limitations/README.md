# Day 191 — AI Applications and Limitations

## Learning Objectives
- Survey major real-world AI applications across Healthcare, Finance, Autonomous Robotics, and NLP.
- Analyze fundamental technical, ethical, and computational limitations of modern AI.
- Examine Moravec's Paradox and the Frame Problem.

## Prerequisites
- Days 173–190 (Phase 1 Understanding AI)

## Topics Covered
- Major AI Application Domains: Medical Diagnosis, Financial Fraud & Trading, Autonomous Vehicles, Natural Language Translation
- Technical Limitations: Hallucination, Brittleness, High Data Dependency, Explainability Black-Box problem
- Moravec's Paradox: "Hard problems (chess) are easy for AI; easy problems (walking, dexterity) are hard for AI"
- The Frame Problem in AI reasoning
