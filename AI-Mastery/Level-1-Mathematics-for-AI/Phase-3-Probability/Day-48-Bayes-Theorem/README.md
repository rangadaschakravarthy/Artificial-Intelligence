# Day 48 — Bayes' Theorem

## Learning Objectives
- Master Bayes' Theorem formula, intuition, and component terms.
- Understand Prior, Likelihood, Posterior, and Marginal Likelihood (Evidence).
- Apply Bayes' Theorem to medical diagnosis, spam filtering, and AI inference.
- Implement Bayesian update calculations in Python.

## Prerequisites
- Days 44–47 Probability rules and Conditional Probability.

## Topics Covered
1. Derivation of Bayes' Theorem
2. Four Pillars: Prior $P(A)$, Likelihood $P(B|A)$, Posterior $P(A|B)$, Evidence $P(B)$
3. Law of Total Probability for Marginal Likelihood
4. Base Rate Fallacy in AI and Medicine
5. Naive Bayes Classification Mathematical Foundations

## Why This Matters for AI
Bayes' Theorem is the fundamental mathematical bridge for updating beliefs in the presence of new data. It underpins Bayesian Machine Learning, Naive Bayes Classifiers, Kalman Filters, Bayesian Neural Networks, and Prompt Evaluation.
