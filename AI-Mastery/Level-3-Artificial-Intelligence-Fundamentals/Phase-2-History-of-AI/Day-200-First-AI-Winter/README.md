# Day 200 — First AI Winter

## Learning Objectives
- Analyze the causes, timeline, and impact of the First AI Winter (1974–1980).
- Examine the Lighthill Report (1973) and Minsky & Papert's *Perceptrons* book (1969).
- Understand the dangers of over-promising and under-delivering in technology cycles.

## Prerequisites
- Day 197: Early AI Research & Logic

## Topics Covered
- What is an "AI Winter"? (Period of reduced funding, institutional skepticism, and stalled research)
- The Lighthill Report (UK Science Research Council, 1973): Combinatorial Explosion critique
- Minsky & Papert's *Perceptrons* (1969): Proved single-layer perceptrons cannot learn XOR function
- Over-promising by early pioneers vs hardware limits of 1970s CPUs/Memory
- Collapse of DARPA & academic AI research funding
