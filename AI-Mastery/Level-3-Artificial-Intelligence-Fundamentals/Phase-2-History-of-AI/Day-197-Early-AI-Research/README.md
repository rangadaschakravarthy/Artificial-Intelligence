# Day 197 — Early AI Research & Logic

## Learning Objectives
- Examine early AI systems (Logic Theorist, General Problem Solver, ELIZA).
- Understand early optimism and the microworlds approach (SHRDLU).
- Analyze early search and logic engines.

## Prerequisites
- Day 196: Dartmouth Conference

## Topics Covered
- **Logic Theorist (1956)**: Proved 38 of 52 theorems from Russell & Whitehead's *Principia Mathematica*
- **General Problem Solver (GPS, 1959)**: Means-Ends Analysis search heuristic
- **ELIZA (1966)**: Joseph Weizenbaum's pattern-matching Rogerian psychotherapist chatbot
- **SHRDLU (1970)**: Terry Winograd's blocks-world natural language manipulation system
- The early conviction that human-level AI was 20 years away
