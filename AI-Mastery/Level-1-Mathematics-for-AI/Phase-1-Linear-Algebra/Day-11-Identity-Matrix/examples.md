# Examples — Identity Matrix

## Example 1 — Very Easy
2x2 Identity: 

$$
\mathbf{I}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Multiplication Check: 

$$
\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ 6 & 7 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ 6 & 7 \end{bmatrix}
$$

.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Kronecker Delta values: $\delta_{1,1} = 1, \delta_{1,2} = 0, \delta_{2,2} = 1$.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Regularization Matrix: 

$$
\lambda \mathbf{I}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
ResNet Shortcut Mapping: $y = f(x) + I x$ preserving feature gradient flow.
