# Day 208 — AI Timeline & Impact

## Learning Objectives
- Analyze key chronological milestones in AI history from 1936 to the present day.
- Understand the societal, economic, and industrial impact of AI breakthroughs.
- Prepare timeline knowledge for technical AI interviews.

## Prerequisites
- Day 207: Modern AI History & Milestone Synthesis

## Topics Covered
- Major Chronological Timeline Milestones (1936 Turing Machine, 1950 Turing Test, 1956 Dartmouth, 1969 Perceptrons, 1973 Lighthill Report, 1986 Backprop, 1997 Deep Blue, 2012 AlexNet, 2016 AlphaGo, 2017 Transformer, 2022 ChatGPT)
- Economic and Labor Market Impact
- Ethical Considerations: Bias, Intellectual Property, Misinformation

## Difficulty
Intermediate
