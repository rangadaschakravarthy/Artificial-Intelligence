# Examples — Derivative Rules

## Example 1 — Very Easy
Sum Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}[3x^2 + 5x] = 6x + 5$.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Product Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}[x \ln(x)] = 1 \cdot \ln(x) + x \cdot \frac{1}{x} = \ln(x) + 1$.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Quotient Rule: $\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{x}{x+1}\right] = \frac{1(x+1) - x(1)}{(x+1)^2} = \frac{1}{(x+1)^2}$.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Sigmoid Derivative: At $x=0$, $\sigma(0)=0.5 \implies \sigma'(0) = 0.5(1-0.5) = 0.25$.
