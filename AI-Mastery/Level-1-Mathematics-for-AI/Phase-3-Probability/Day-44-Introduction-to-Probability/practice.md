# Practice Problems — Introduction to Probability

## Level 1: Basic Concept Checks
1. What is the valid numerical range for any probability $P(A)$?
2. If $P(A) = 0.35$, what is $P(	ext{not } A)$?
3. State Kolmogorov's Second Axiom.
4. True or False: Empirical probability always equals theoretical probability for a small sample size.
5. What is the sample space $\Omega$ when flipping 2 coins?

## Level 2: Direct Calculations
6. What is the probability of rolling a number greater than 4 on a fair 6-sided die?
7. In a class of 30 students, 18 study Python. What is the probability that a randomly chosen student studies Python?
8. What is the probability of picking a red marble from a bag of 5 red, 3 blue, and 2 green marbles?
9. A bag contains 10 balls numbered 1 to 10. What is the probability of picking a prime number?
10. Calculate $P(	ext{Heads})$ if a biased coin lands on Tails 70 times out of 100 trials.

## Level 3: Conceptual & Multi-Step Problems
11. Explain how the Law of Large Numbers connects empirical probability to theoretical probability.
12. If $P(A) = 0.4$, $P(B) = 0.3$, and $A$ and $B$ are mutually exclusive, find $P(A \cup B)$.
13. Show that if $A \subseteq B$, then $P(A) \le P(B)$.
14. A weather model states $P(	ext{Rain}) = 0.70$. What are the odds in favor of rain?
15. If a system fails when either Component 1 or Component 2 fails (disjoint failures with probabilities 0.02 and 0.03), find system failure probability.

## Level 4: AI & ML Applications
16. A multi-class model outputs raw logits $[3.0, 1.0, 0.0]$. Compute the Softmax probability for class 1.
17. A spam detection model has precision 0.90 on flagged emails. If 100 emails are flagged, how many true spam emails are expected?
18. Explain why raw neural network outputs must be transformed into probabilities before computing Cross-Entropy loss.
19. Given predicted probability $\hat{y} = 0.85$ and true label $y = 1$, calculate binary cross-entropy loss $-\log(\hat{y})$.
20. In an A/B test, Variant A has conversion probability 0.12 and Variant B has 0.15. If 1000 users visit each, find expected total conversions.

## Level 5: Interview Questions
21. What is the difference between Frequentist and Bayesian views of probability?
22. Prove $P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)$ using Kolmogorov Axioms.
23. Why is Softmax preferred over simple normalization $\frac{x_i}{\sum x_j}$ for converting neural network outputs to probabilities?
