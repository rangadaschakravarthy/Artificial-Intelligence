# Day 191 Theory: AI Applications and Limitations

### 1. What Is It?
AI Applications represent the deployment of intelligent algorithms to solve real-world industry tasks, while AI Limitations catalog the computational, logical, and safety boundaries that constrain modern systems.

### 2. Moravec's Paradox
Discovered by Hans Moravec, Rodney Brooks, and Marvin Minsky in the 1980s:
> High-level reasoning (playing chess or proving logic theorems) requires very little computation, whereas low-level sensorimotor skills (walking, perception, dexterity) require massive computational resources and complex neural architectures.

### 3. Key Limitations of Modern AI
- **Black-Box Explainability**: Deep networks make accurate predictions without human-interpretable reasoning logs.
- **Hallucination & Bias**: Probabilistic models output confident incorrect assertions.
- **Data & Compute Limits**: Training requires gigawatts of energy and petabytes of data.

### 4. Summary
AI excels at complex high-dimensional pattern matching but encounters Moravec's Paradox and explainability boundaries.
