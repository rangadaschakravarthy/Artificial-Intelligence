# Day 199 — Expert Systems

## Learning Objectives
- Understand Expert Systems (MYCIN, DENDRAL, XCON) that dominated 1980s commercial AI.
- Learn Expert System Architecture including Explanation Facilities and Conflict Resolution.
- Analyze the commercial success and eventual Knowledge Acquisition Bottleneck limitations.

## Prerequisites
- Day 198: Symbolic AI and Knowledge Bases

## Topics Covered
- Expert Systems Definition (Emulating human expert decision-making in specialized domains)
- Historical Systems:
  - **DENDRAL (1965)**: Organic chemistry mass spectrometry analysis
  - **MYCIN (1972)**: Bacterial infection blood diagnosis with certainty factors
  - **XCON/R1 (1978)**: VAX computer hardware configuration system for DEC
- Expert System Components: Knowledge Base, Inference Engine, Explanation Subsystem, User Interface
- Certainty Factors & Handling Uncertainty in Rule Engines

Intermediate
