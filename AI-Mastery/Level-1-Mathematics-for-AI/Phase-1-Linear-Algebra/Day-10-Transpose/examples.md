# Examples — Transpose

## Example 1 — Very Easy
2x2 Transpose: 

$$
\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}^T = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
$$

.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Row Vector to Column Vector: 

$$
[1, 2, 3]^T = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}
$$

.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Symmetric Matrix Check: 

$$
\mathbf{S} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 2 \\ 2 & 9 \end{bmatrix} \implies \mathbf{S}^T = \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 2 \\ 2 & 9 \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{S}
$$

.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Gram Matrix \mathbf{A}^T \mathbf{A}: 

$$
\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \implies \mathbf{A}^T \mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 3 & 14 \end{bmatrix}
$$

 (Symmetric!).
