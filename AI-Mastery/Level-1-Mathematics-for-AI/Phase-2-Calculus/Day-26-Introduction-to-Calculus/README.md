- Distinguish between Differential Calculus (rates of change) and Integral Calculus (accumulation)

## Prerequisites
Basic Algebra and Arithmetic

## Topics Covered
- What is Calculus and why AI needs it
- Differential Calculus vs Integral Calculus high-level overview
- The concept of continuous change vs discrete steps
- Slope of a secant line vs tangent line
- How machine learning algorithms optimize error using calculus

## Why This Matters for AI
Machine learning is fundamentally about continuous optimization. We define an error/loss curve, and calculus tells us which direction and how far to adjust model weights to reduce error.
