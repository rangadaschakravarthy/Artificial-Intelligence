3. Which measure works on Nominal data?

5. What loss function yields the Median as its optimal prediction?

## Level 2: Calculation

8. Compute harmonic mean of 40 mph and 60 mph over equal distance.

14. Explain why mean = median = mode in a symmetric unimodal distribution.
15. How does positive skewness affect relative positions of mean, median, mode?
