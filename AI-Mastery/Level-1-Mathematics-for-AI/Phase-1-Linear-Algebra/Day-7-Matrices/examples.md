# Examples — Matrices

## Example 1 — Very Easy
2x3 Matrix: 

$$
\mathbf{A} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \end{bmatrix}
$$

, shape 2 \times 3.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Square Matrix (3x3): 

$$
\mathbf{B} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 & 5 \\ 6 & 7 & 8 \end{bmatrix}
$$

, m=n=3.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Diagonal Matrix: 

$$
\mathbf{D} = \text{diag}(10, 20, 30) = \begin{bmatrix} 10 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 20 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 30 \end{bmatrix}
$$

.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Symmetric Matrix: 

$$
\mathbf{S} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 4 & 5 \\ 4 & 2 & 6 \\ 5 & 6 & 3 \end{bmatrix}
$$

, where S_{i,j} = S_{j,i}.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Dataset Matrix: 4 housing samples with 2 features each (SqFt, Bedrooms).
