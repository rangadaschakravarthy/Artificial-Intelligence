# Day 206 — Generative AI Revolution

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the rapid emergence of commercial Generative AI (2020–Present).
- Examine ChatGPT, Diffusion Image Generators, and Multimodal Foundation Models.
- Trace the historical transition from specialized task models to general Foundation Models.

## Prerequisites
- Day 204: Deep Learning Revolution
- Day 205: Transformer Revolution

## Topics Covered
- Generative AI Era (2020–Present): Transition from single-task classification to open-ended content generation
- Key Breakthrough Milestones:
  - **GPT-3 (2020)**: Few-shot learning at 175 billion parameters
  - **Diffusion Models (2021–2022)**: DALL-E 2, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion
  - **ChatGPT (Nov 2022)**: RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) making conversational LLMs accessible
  - **Multimodal Models (2023–Present)**: GPT-4V, Gemini processing text, vision, audio natively
- Definition of Foundation Models (Bommasani et al. 2021)
