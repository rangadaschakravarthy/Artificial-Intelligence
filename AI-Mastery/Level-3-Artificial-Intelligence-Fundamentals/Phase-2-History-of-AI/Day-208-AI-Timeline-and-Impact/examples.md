# Day 208 Worked Examples: AI Timeline & Impact

## Example 1 — Practical: Chronological Milestone Query Engine
```python
timeline = {
    1950: "Turing Test proposed by Alan Turing",
    1956: "Dartmouth Conference coins 'Artificial Intelligence'",
    1997: "IBM Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov",
    2012: "AlexNet wins ImageNet, sparking Deep Learning revolution",
    2016: "DeepMind AlphaGo defeats Go champion Lee Sedol",
    2017: "Transformers introduced ('Attention Is All You Need')",
    2022: "ChatGPT released, popularizing Generative AI globally"
}

for year, event in timeline.items():
    print(f"Year {year}: {event}")
```
