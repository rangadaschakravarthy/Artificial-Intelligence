# Day 201 — Second AI Winter

## Learning Objectives
- Analyze the causes, timeline, and collapse of the 1980s Expert Systems boom during the Second AI Winter (1987–1993).
- Examine the Lisp Machine market collapse and specialized hardware obsolescence.
- Extract enduring lessons on technology Hype Cycles.

## Prerequisites
- Day 199: Expert Systems
- Day 200: First AI Winter

## Topics Covered
- The 1980s Expert Systems Commercial Boom (XCON, Lisp Machines Inc, Symbolics)
- The Second AI Winter Timeline (1987–1993)
- Catalyst 1: Collapse of the specialized LISP Machine market (Commodity x86 PCs and Sun Workstations surpassed specialized hardware)
- Catalyst 2: High maintenance costs and extreme brittleness of large hand-coded rule bases
- The Gartner Hype Cycle: Peak of Inflated Expectations $\to$ Trough of Disillusionment
