# Day 189 Theory: Deep Learning vs Generative AI

### 1. What Is It?
Deep Learning is the underlying neural network technology and optimization framework. Generative AI is a specific application capability *built using* deep learning architectures to generate novel synthetic content.

### 2. Paradigm Distinctions
- **Discriminative Deep Learning**: Uses deep neural networks (CNNs, ResNets) to classify, detect, or segment existing data ($P(Y \mid X)$).
- **Generative AI**: Uses deep neural architectures (Transformers, Diffusion, GANs) to synthesize new data samples ($P(X)$).

### 3. Summary
Deep Learning is the neural network technology stack; Generative AI is the content-creation capability built on top of deep learning.
