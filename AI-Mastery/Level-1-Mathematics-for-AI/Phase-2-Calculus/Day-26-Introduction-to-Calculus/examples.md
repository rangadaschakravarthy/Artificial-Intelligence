# Examples — Introduction to Calculus

## Example 1 — Very Easy
Secant Line Slope: $f(x) = x^2$ between $x=1$ and $x=3 \implies \text{Slope} = \frac{9-1}{3-1} = 4$.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Approaching Tangent: $x=1$ and $x=1.001 \implies f(1.001)=1.002001 \implies \text{Slope} = \frac{0.002001}{0.001} = 2.001 \approx 2$.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Loss Curve Minimum: $L(w) = (w - 3)^2$. At $w=5$, slope $= 4 > 0 \implies$ decrease $w$ to reduce loss!

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Constant Slope: $f(x) = 5x - 2 \implies$ slope is constant 5 everywhere.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Speedometer Analogy: Distance function $s(t)$, velocity $v(t) = s'(t)$.
