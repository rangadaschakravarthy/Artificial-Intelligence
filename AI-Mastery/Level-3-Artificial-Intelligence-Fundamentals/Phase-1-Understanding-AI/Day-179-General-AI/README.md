# Day 179 — General AI (AGI Concept)

## Learning Objectives
- Understand Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) as a theoretical concept.
- Distinguish between modern Narrow AI and theoretical AGI capabilities.
- Evaluate criteria for general intelligence (cross-domain transfer, autonomous reasoning, common-sense reasoning).

## Prerequisites
- Day 178: Narrow AI

## Topics Covered
- Definition of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI / Strong AI)
- Key Capabilities of AGI: Cross-domain transfer learning, common-sense reasoning, abstract thinking, self-directed learning
- Benchmarks proposed for AGI: Coffee Test (Wozniak), Employment Test (Nilsson), College Student Test (Goertzel)
- AGI vs ANI comparative analysis
- Current state of research: Theoretical models vs empirical realities

## Difficulty
Intermediate
