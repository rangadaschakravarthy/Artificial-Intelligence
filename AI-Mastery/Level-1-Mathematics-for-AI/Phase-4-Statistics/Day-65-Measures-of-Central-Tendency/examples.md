# Day 65 Worked Examples: Measures of Central Tendency

## Example 1: Very Easy — Basic Mean, Median, Mode
Data: [1, 3, 3, 6, 7].
Mean = 20/5 = 4. Median = 3. Mode = 3.

## Example 2: Beginner — Impact of Outliers
Data: [10, 12, 14, 15, 1000].
Mean = 1051/5 = 210.2. Median = 14. Outlier skewed mean by +196 units!

## Example 3: Intermediate — Geometric Mean of Investment Returns
Returns over 3 years: +10% (1.10), +50% (1.50), -20% (0.80).
G = (1.10 * 1.50 * 0.80)^(1/3) = (1.32)^(1/3) ≈ 1.097 (9.7% per year).

## Example 4: AI Focus — F1-Score Computation
Precision = 0.90, Recall = 0.30.
F1 = 2 * (0.9 * 0.3) / (0.9 + 0.3) = 0.54 / 1.2 = 0.45.

## Example 5: Real-World AI — Robust Center for Imbalanced Loss
In house price prediction with extreme mansion prices, training with MAE loss causes network to output median price, avoiding high-variance predictions.
