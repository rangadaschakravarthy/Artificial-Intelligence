# Day 1 — Introduction to Linear Algebra

## Learning Objectives
- Understand what Linear Algebra is and why it is the backbone of AI
- Distinguish between scalar, vector, matrix, and tensor concepts high-level
- Visualize algebraic operations geometrically
- Recognize how AI systems process real-world data mathematically

## Prerequisites
Basic high-school arithmetic and algebra (addition, multiplication, variables).

## Topics Covered
- Definition and scope of Linear Algebra
- The core mathematical objects: Scalars, Vectors, Matrices, Tensors
- Geometric vs Algebraic views of data
- Why computers and AI models operate on linear algebra

## Why This Matters for AI
AI algorithms do not process words, images, or audio directly; they process numbers organized in vectors and matrices. Linear algebra allows us to manipulate millions of data points simultaneously.
