# Quick Revision Cheat Sheet

- **6-Tuple**: $\langle S, s_0, A, T, G, c \rangle$
- **BFS**: $O(b^d)$ time/space, FIFO, unweighted optimal.
- **DFS**: $O(bm)$ space, LIFO stack, incomplete in infinite spaces.
- **UCS**: Min-Priority Queue by $g(n)$, weighted optimal.
- **A***: $f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$, optimal if $h$ admissible.
- **Alpha-Beta**: Prunes when $\alpha \ge \beta$, best case $O(b^{m/2})$.
