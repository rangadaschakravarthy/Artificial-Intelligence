# Day 195 — The Turing Test

## Learning Objectives
- Understand Alan Turing's 1950 Imitation Game (The Turing Test).
- Examine common misconceptions and philosophical criticisms (Searle's Chinese Room argument).
- Evaluate the relevance of the Turing Test in the era of Large Language Models (LLMs).

## Prerequisites
- Day 194: Turing and Computation

## Topics Covered
- Alan Turing's 1950 Paper: *Computing Machinery and Intelligence* ("Can machines think?")
- The Imitation Game Setup: Human Evaluator, Human Interlocutor, Machine Interlocutor
- What the Turing Test actually measures (Operational behavioral imitation, not consciousness)
- Philosophical Criticisms: John Searle's Chinese Room Gedankenexperiment (Syntax vs Semantics)
- Modern Context: Why passing textual conversation (e.g. LLMs) does not prove general intelligence

## Difficulty
Intermediate
