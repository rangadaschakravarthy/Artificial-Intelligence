# Day 174 — What is Artificial Intelligence

## Learning Objectives
- Explore historical, computer science, engineering, ML, and agent perspectives on AI.
- Understand why no single universally accepted definition of AI exists.
- Evaluate criteria for machine intelligence.

## Prerequisites
- Day 173: Introduction to AI

## Topics Covered
- 5 Perspectives on AI: Historical, CS, Engineering, ML, and Agent
- The Dartmouth 1956 conjecture ("Every aspect of learning or intelligence can be precisely described...")
- Intelligence metrics: Adaptation, generalization, reasoning, speed
- Why definitions evolve as technology advances (The "AI Effect")

## Completion Checklist
- [ ] I can articulate 5 different perspectives on AI.
- [ ] I understand the AI Effect (once an AI problem is solved, it's called computation).
- [ ] I can evaluate systems against intelligence benchmarks.
