# Day 173 — Introduction to AI

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the multi-faceted definitions and scopes of Artificial Intelligence.
- Contrast human intelligence with artificial systems.
- Explore the relationship between mathematical optimization, data, and intelligent systems.

## Prerequisites
- Level 1: Mathematics for AI
- Level 2: Data Science Foundations

## Topics Covered
- Multiple perspectives on AI: Historical, CS, Engineering, ML, and Agent views
- Thought vs Action: Thinking Humanly, Thinking Rationally, Acting Humanly, Acting Rationally
- Intelligence as problem-solving, perception, and adaptation
- Core capabilities required for AI systems

## Why This Matters
Establishing a precise definition of AI prevents confusing rule-based automation with genuine intelligent behavior.

## Real-World Usage
Defining AI criteria for software audit, compliance, and enterprise automation assessment.

## Study Order
1. Read `theory.md` for AI definitions.
2. Review `examples.md` for domain classification.
3. Run `code.py` to see rule vs intelligent agent simulation.
4. Complete `practice.md` and verify with `solution.md`.

## Completion Checklist
- [ ] I can explain the 4 quadrants of AI definitions (Thinking/Acting Humanly/Rationally).
- [ ] I can distinguish between artificial systems and human cognition.
- [ ] I understand why no single definition captures all of AI.
