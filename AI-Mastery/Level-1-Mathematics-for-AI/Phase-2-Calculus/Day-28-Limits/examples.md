# Examples — Limits

## Example 1 — Very Easy
Direct Substitution Limit: $\lim_{x \to 3} (2x + 1) = 2(3) + 1 = 7$.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Factoring Indeterminate Limit: $\lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^2 - 1}{x - 1} = \lim_{x \to 1} (x + 1) = 2$.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
L'Hôpital's Rule Limit: $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\cos(x)}{1} = 1$.

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Infinite Limit: $\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1}{x} = 0$.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Numerical Stability Guard: $\ln(\max(\epsilon, p))$ preventing $\ln(0) \to -\infty$.
