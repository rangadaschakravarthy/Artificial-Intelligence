# Day 179 Theory: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

### 1. What Is It?
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), also known as Strong AI or Full AI, is a theoretical form of AI that possesses human-like general cognitive abilities, enabling it to understand, learn, and apply knowledge across any intellectual task that a human can perform.

### 2. AGI vs Narrow AI Comparison
| Dimension | Narrow AI (ANI) | General AI (AGI) |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Single specialized task | Any intellectual task |
| **Transfer Learning** | Zero or minimal | Autonomous cross-domain transfer |
| **Common Sense** | None | Robust common-sense reasoning |
| **Current Status** | 100% Deployed Reality | Theoretical Research Concept |

### 3. Core AGI Criteria
- **Transfer**: Applying lessons learned in chess or language to physics or motor robotics autonomously.
- **Common Sense**: Understanding basic physical and social cause-effect dynamics without explicit rule training.

### 4. Summary
AGI remains a theoretical research concept representing human-level general cross-domain cognitive capability.
