# Day 180 — Superintelligence Concept

## Learning Objectives
- Understand Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) as defined in AI philosophy (Bostrom).
- Analyze the Intelligence Explosion concept and recursive self-improvement hypothesis.
- Explore AI alignment and safety considerations.

## Prerequisites
- Day 179: General AI

## Topics Covered
- Definition of Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)
- Nick Bostrom's definition: Intellect that vastly outperforms the best human brains in every field
- Recursive Self-Improvement & The Intelligence Explosion hypothesis (I.J. Good)
- AI Alignment Problem: Ensuring superintelligent goals align with human values
- Orthogonality Thesis & Instrumental Convergence concepts
