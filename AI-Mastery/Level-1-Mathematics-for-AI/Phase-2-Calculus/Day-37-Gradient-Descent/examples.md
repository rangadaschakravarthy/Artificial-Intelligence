# Examples — Gradient Descent

## Example 1 — Very Easy
1D GD Optimization: Minimizing $L(w) = w^2$ from $w=5$ to $w \to 0$.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Batch GD: Computing average gradient across all 10,000 samples before updating weights.

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Stochastic GD (SGD): Updating weights after every single sample (10,000 updates per epoch).

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Mini-Batch GD: Updating weights after every mini-batch of 64 samples (156 updates per epoch).

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Epochs vs Iterations: $N=10,000, B=64 \implies 156$ iterations per epoch.
