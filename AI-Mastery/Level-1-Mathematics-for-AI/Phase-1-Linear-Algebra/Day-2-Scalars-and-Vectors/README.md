# Day 2 — Scalars and Vectors

## Learning Objectives
- Master the distinction between scalars (0D tensors) and vectors (1D tensors)
- Understand vector dimension, magnitude, and direction
- Represent column vectors and row vectors geometrically and algebraically
- Map real-world data points to feature vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$

## Prerequisites
Day 1 — Introduction to Linear Algebra

## Topics Covered
- Scalars vs Vectors
- Vector notation and column/row representations
- Vector dimension ($n$-dimensional space $\mathbb{R}^n$)
- Geometric visualization of 2D/3D vectors
- Feature vectors in ML

## Why This Matters for AI
Machine learning models treat individual observations (e.g. an image, a sentence embedding, a user vector) as points or arrows in high-dimensional space.
