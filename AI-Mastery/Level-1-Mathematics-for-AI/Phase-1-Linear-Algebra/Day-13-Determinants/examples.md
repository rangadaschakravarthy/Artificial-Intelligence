# Examples — Determinants

## Example 1 — Very Easy
2x2 Determinant: 

$$\det \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 2 \\ 1 & 3 \end{bmatrix} = 5(3) - 2(1) = 15 - 2 = 13$$

.

## Example 2 — Beginner
Diagonal Matrix Determinant: $\det \text{diag}(2, 3, 4) = 2 \times 3 \times 4 = 24$ (Product of diagonal elements!).

## Example 3 — Intermediate
Singular Matrix Area Collapse: 

$$\det \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix} = 4 - 4 = 0$$

 (Collapse to line).

## Example 4 — AI/ML Example
Product Determinant Property: $\det(\mathbf{A}) = 3, \det(\mathbf{B}) = 4 \implies \det(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}) = 12$.

## Example 5 — Real-World Interpretation
Multivariate Normal Density: Covariance matrix determinant $|\mathbf{\Sigma}|$ scaling probability volume.
