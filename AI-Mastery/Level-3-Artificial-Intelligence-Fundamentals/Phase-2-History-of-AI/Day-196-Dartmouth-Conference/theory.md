# Day 196 Theory: Dartmouth Conference (1956)

### 1. What Is It?
The Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence, held in August 1956 at Dartmouth College, is universally recognized as the founding event of Artificial Intelligence as an independent academic discipline.

### 2. Key Attendees & Contributions
- **John McCarthy**: Coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" and created the LISP programming language.
- **Marvin Minsky**: Pioneer of frame representations and early neural network perceptor critique.
- **Claude Shannon**: Founder of Information Theory ($H = -\sum p_i \log_2 p_i$).
- **Allen Newell & Herbert Simon**: Demonstrated the *Logic Theorist*, the first working AI program proving logic theorems.

### 3. Summary
The 1956 Dartmouth Conference formally established Artificial Intelligence as an academic discipline.
