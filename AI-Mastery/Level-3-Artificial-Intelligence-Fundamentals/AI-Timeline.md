# AI Timeline & Historical Milestones

| Period | Milestone Event | What Happened | Historical Impact & Significance |
| ------ | --------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **1950** | Turing Test | Alan Turing publishes "Computing Machinery and Intelligence". | Proposed the Imitation Game as a functional benchmark for machine intelligence. |
| **1956** | Dartmouth Workshop | John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester, and Claude Shannon organize conference. | Formally coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" and established AI as an academic discipline. |
| **1966** | ELIZA Chatbot | Joseph Weizenbaum creates natural language pattern matching bot. | Showed early illusion of empathy and conversation via simple rule parsing. |
| **1974-80** | First AI Winter | Government funding (DARPA/Lighthill Report) dropped drastically. | Caused by overpromised capabilities and combinatorial state explosion limitations. |
| **1980s** | Expert Systems Boom | Commercial adoption of rule-based systems like MYCIN and R1 (XCON). | Restored industry interest and demonstrated corporate value of symbolic AI knowledge bases. |
| **1987-93** | Second AI Winter | Collapse of specialized Lisp machine market and expert system maintenance bottlenecks. | Highlighted fragility of hand-coded rules and brittleness under non-deterministic noise. |
| **1997** | Deep Blue Defeats Kasparov | IBM's Deep Blue defeats World Chess Champion Garry Kasparov. | Proved power of high-speed hardware alpha-beta search and specialized evaluation functions. |
| **2012** | AlexNet ImageNet Victory | Geoffrey Hinton, Alex Krizhevsky, and Ilya Sutskever win ImageNet using Deep CNNs. | Ignited the Deep Learning Revolution powered by GPUs and massive datasets. |
| **2017** | Transformer Architecture | Vaswani et al. publish "Attention Is All You Need". | Replaced recurrent networks with parallel self-attention, unlocking Modern Generative AI and LLMs. |
| **2022+** | Generative AI & Foundation Models | OpenAI releases ChatGPT; emergence of Multimodal LLMs and Reasoning Models. | Shifted AI from narrow classification task solvers to broad conversational and generative agents. |
