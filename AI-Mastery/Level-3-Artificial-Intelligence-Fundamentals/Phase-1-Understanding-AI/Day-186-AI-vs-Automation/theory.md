# Day 186 Theory: AI vs Automation

### 1. What Is It?
Automation refers to using technology to perform predictable, repetitive tasks without human intervention according to fixed rules. Artificial Intelligence refers to systems that perceive environments, make decisions under uncertainty, and adapt.

### 2. Comparative Matrix
| Dimension | Automation (RPA / Scripts) | Artificial Intelligence |
|---|---|---|
| **Rules Source** | Hand-coded by human developers | Learned from data or derived via search |
| **Adaptability** | Zero — fails on unexpected inputs | High — generalizes to novel inputs |
| **Input Data** | Strictly structured (CSV, SQL) | Unstructured (Images, Text, Audio) |
| **Uncertainty** | Cannot handle ambiguity | Probabilistic decision-making |

### 3. Summary
Automation executes fixed repetitive scripts; AI perceives, decides under uncertainty, and adapts.
