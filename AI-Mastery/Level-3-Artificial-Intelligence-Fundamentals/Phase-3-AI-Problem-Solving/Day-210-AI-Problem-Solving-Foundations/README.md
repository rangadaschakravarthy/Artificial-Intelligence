## Why This Matters in AI
Before an AI agent can execute search, optimization, or learning algorithms, it must abstract real-world environments into mathematical graph structures. Problem formulation is the foundational step of modern AI engineering.

## Real-World AI Usage
- Autonomous vehicle route generation (abstracting city roads into weighted graphs).
- Automated theorem provers and game bots (representing moves as valid state transitions).
