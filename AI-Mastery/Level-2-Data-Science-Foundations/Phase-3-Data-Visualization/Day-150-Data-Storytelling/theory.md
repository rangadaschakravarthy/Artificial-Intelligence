# Day 150 Theory: Data Storytelling

### 1. What Is It?
Data Storytelling is the practice of combining data visualizations, narrative context, and strategic focal design to communicate insights clearly and drive action.

### 2. Core Principles
1. **Maximize Data-to-Ink Ratio**: Remove non-data gridlines, borders, and background fills.
2. **Strategic Color**: Use neutral gray for baseline context and a single bold accent color for the key insight point.
3. **Action Titles**: Replace descriptive titles (`"Sales by Month"`) with insight headers (`"March Promo Boosted Monthly Sales by 40%"`).

### 3. Summary
Data Storytelling uses clean design, action-oriented titles, and accent colors to guide stakeholder attention directly to actionable insights.
