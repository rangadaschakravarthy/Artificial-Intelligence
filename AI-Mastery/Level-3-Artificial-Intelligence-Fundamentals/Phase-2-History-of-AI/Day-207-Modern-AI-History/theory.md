# Day 207 Theory: Modern AI History & Milestone Synthesis

### 1. What Is It?
Modern AI History is characterized by alternating waves of symbolic rule-based reasoning and statistical connectionist neural networks, driven by hardware speed, dataset scale, and algorithmic breakthroughs.

### 2. The AI Pendulum Framework
```
1956 (Dartmouth) ➔ 1974 (First Winter) ➔ 1980 (Expert Systems) ➔ 1987 (Second Winter) ➔ 1995 (Statistical ML) ➔ 2012 (Deep Learning) ➔ 2017 (Transformers) ➔ 2022 (GenAI)
```

### 3. Summary
AI history reveals that technological breakthroughs occur when theoretical algorithms align with sufficient data scale and GPU hardware compute.
