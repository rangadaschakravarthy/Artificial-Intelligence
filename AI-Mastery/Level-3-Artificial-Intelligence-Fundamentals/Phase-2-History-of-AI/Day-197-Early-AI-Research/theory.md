# Day 197 Theory: Early AI Research & Logic

### 1. What Is It?
Early AI research (1956–1970s) focused on symbolic logic, theorem proving, search heuristics, and constrained "microworlds"—leading to high early optimism.

### 2. Key Early Programs
- **Logic Theorist (Newell & Simon)**: Proved mathematical logic theorems using search trees.
- **General Problem Solver (GPS)**: Introduced **Means-Ends Analysis**—calculating difference vectors between current state and goal state and applying operators to reduce the difference.
- **ELIZA (Weizenbaum)**: Used simple string substitution regex pattern matching to simulate a therapist, demonstrating how easily humans attribute real intelligence to basic scripts.

### 3. Summary
Early AI established means-ends analysis, theorem proving, and pattern matching in constrained domains.
